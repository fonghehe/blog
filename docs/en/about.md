## About this blog

This blog collects learning notes, technical explanations and approaches to frontend development. Articles aim to make the problem, assumptions and references explicit; the archive preserves historical context.

Public author profile: [fonghehe](https://github.com/fonghehe). Source and feedback: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/fonghehe/blog).

## Editorial policy {#content-policy}

- Publication dates preserve the original timeline. Later revisions have a separate update date and should not be presented as knowledge available at the original date.
- A verification date is added only after checking the relevant behavior. Its absence does not imply verification. A version field identifies the scope discussed.
- Archived articles may use old versions or APIs. Check current official documentation before applying an example.
- Teaching examples, hypothetical scenarios and personal experience must be distinguished. Production or performance claims require the environment, method and supporting evidence.
- Historical content is under review. Repetitive wording, example relevance and translation quality still need attention; these site improvements do not mean every article has been verified.

## Report an issue

Use an article comment or GitHub issue with the article URL, passage, applicable version and official reference. Do not post credentials or personal data in public comments.
